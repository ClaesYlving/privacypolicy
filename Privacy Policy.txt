Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2 November 2026
1. Introduction
Keylinx AB ("Keylinx", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data when you use our services, including functionality that allows professional profile information to be imported, analyzed, structured, and used within our platform.
2. Information We Process
Depending on how you use our services, we may process information such as:
•	Name
•	Professional title
•	Employment history
•	Education
•	Skills and competencies
•	Certifications
•	Professional experience
•	Profile description
•	Publicly available professional information
•	Information provided directly by you
•	Information imported by you from third-party services
•	Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service
Where information originates from LinkedIn, we only process such information where it has been provided by the user or obtained through a method permitted by LinkedIn and applicable law.
3. How We Use Personal Data
We may use personal data to:
•	Create and maintain professional profiles
•	Identify and structure skills, competencies, education, and work experience
•	Improve profile information and searchability
•	Match professional profiles against assignments, projects, or competence requirements
•	Provide semantic search and matching functionality
•	Operate, secure, and improve our services
•	Provide customer support
•	Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements
Where automated technologies, including artificial intelligence and semantic matching, are used, they may assist in analyzing professional information and identifying relationships between skills, experience, and requirements.
4. LinkedIn Data
LinkedIn is a third-party service operated independently from Keylinx.
Our service is not intended to circumvent LinkedIn's technical restrictions, access controls, or terms of service.
Where LinkedIn information is processed through our service, the user is responsible for ensuring that they have the right to provide or import that information.
We do not claim ownership of information originating from LinkedIn.
LinkedIn's own terms and privacy policies continue to apply to the use of LinkedIn and information obtained through its services.
5. Legal Basis
Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process personal data only when a valid legal basis exists.
Depending on the circumstances, this may include:
•	Consent, where you have agreed to specific processing.
•	Contract, where processing is required to provide a service requested by you.
•	Legitimate interests, where processing is necessary for legitimate business purposes and those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
•	Legal obligation, where processing is necessary for us to comply with applicable law.
6. AI and Semantic Processing
Our services may use automated processing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, embeddings, or similar technologies to analyze professional information.
For example, the service may identify that differently worded descriptions relate to similar skills or experience.
Such processing may be used to:
•	Categorize skills
•	Structure CV/profile information
•	Improve search results
•	Calculate relevance between profiles and assignments
•	Suggest skills or competencies associated with professional experience
Where required by applicable law, individuals may request information about automated processing affecting their personal data.
7. Sharing of Personal Data
We may share personal data with service providers that help us operate our platform, such as providers of:
•	Cloud hosting
•	Data storage
•	Authentication
•	Artificial intelligence services
•	Monitoring and security services
These providers may only process personal data according to our instructions and applicable data-protection requirements where they act as our processors.
We do not sell personal data.
8. International Data Transfers
Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), we use appropriate safeguards as required under applicable data-protection law.
Where applicable, these safeguards may include the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another legally recognized transfer mechanism.
9. Data Retention
We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by law.
When personal information is no longer necessary, it will be deleted or anonymized in accordance with our retention procedures.
10. Your Rights
Where GDPR applies, you may have the right to:
•	Request access to your personal data
•	Correct inaccurate information
•	Request deletion of your personal data
•	Restrict certain processing
•	Object to processing based on legitimate interests
•	Request data portability
•	Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
•	Lodge a complaint with a relevant data-protection authority
Requests concerning personal data can be sent using the contact details below.
11. Security
We use appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.
Access to personal data is limited to individuals and service providers that require access for legitimate purposes.
12. Third-Party Services
Our services may interact with or contain links to third-party services such as LinkedIn.
Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.
13. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal obligations change.
The current version will be made available through our service or website together with its latest revision date.
